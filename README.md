# ASSISTIVE

### Assistive Health & Emergency Safety

> **One tap. Campus alerted. Help in motion.**

ASSISTIVE is a campus health and emergency safety platform designed to connect students with nearby campus responders during urgent situations.

Instead of relying on a student to find the correct phone number, explain their location, or manually contact multiple people, ASSISTIVE provides a simple emergency workflow:

**One-Tap SOS → Campus Alert → First Responder → AI Guidance → Resolution**

The project was built as a lightweight hackathon prototype with a strong focus on speed, accessibility, realtime coordination, and practical AI assistance.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

During a campus emergency, students may:

- Panic and struggle to explain what happened.
- Not know which campus authority to contact.
- Waste time searching for medical or security numbers.
- Be unable to communicate effectively during an emergency.
- Have no visibility into whether someone is responding.

At the same time, faculty or campus responders may receive incomplete information and have difficulty coordinating the initial response.

ASSISTIVE addresses this by creating a shared emergency communication layer between students and campus responders.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

ASSISTIVE provides students with two primary actions:

### 1. ONE-TAP SOS

A student can quickly trigger an emergency alert containing:

- Student identity
- Emergency type
- Campus location
- Timestamp
- Optional note
- Incident ID

The alert is immediately propagated to connected campus users.

### 2. CAMPUS MEDICAL HELP

Students can quickly access:

- Campus medical assistance
- Emergency/security contact
- Call actions
- Relevant campus help information
